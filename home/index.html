BEFORE YOUR FIRST CONVERSATION

Three small checks.
A more useful reading.

  1. Know your question.

    Choose something you want to reflect on. Focus on decisions you can make, rather than certainty about someone else’s feelings.

  2. Set your boundaries.

    Decide on a budget and session length. Check how the provider bills, and whether you can stop when you choose.

  3. Leave room for your judgment.

    A reading offers a perspective. You decide what is useful, what to set aside and whether to continue.

GOOD QUESTIONS TO ASK

A little clarity
before you book.

How were these readers chosen?

We looked for documented provider profiles with a photograph, stated specialties and enough information to compare reading styles. The lists show a range of approaches; they do not measure psychic accuracy.

Have you personally tested these readers?

No. We describe information from provider profiles and link to its source. We do not claim first-hand readings, independent vetting or verified outcomes.

Are the prices and schedules current?

Check the provider’s page for current rates, offers, formats and availability. Our saved profile information is a reference, not a live booking feed.

Can a reading predict a relationship outcome?

A reading cannot establish another person’s private feelings or guarantee a reunion. Respect other people’s boundaries and make decisions using observable actions and your own judgment.
