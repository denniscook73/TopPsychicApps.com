    Our Full Review

    Psychic Source Overview

    Psychic Source has been operating since 1989, making it one of the longest-running psychic services available. The platform is particularly renowned for its love and relationship reading specialists.

    Love Reading Expertise

    While Psychic Source covers all specialties, it has built a strong reputation specifically for love and relationship guidance. Many of their top-rated advisors specialize in romantic connections, compatibility, and relationship healing.

    Getting Started

    New users benefit from heavily discounted introductory rates, sometimes as low as $1 per minute. This makes it easy to try multiple advisors before committing to a regular reader.

    Our Verdict

    Psychic Source’s three decades of operation prove its reliability. The strong focus on love readings and affordable introductory pricing make it an excellent choice for those seeking romantic guidance.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is Psychic Source safe?

    Yes. It is the oldest continuously-trading psychic network in the US (since 1989), BBB-accredited with A+, and offers an industry-best satisfaction guarantee. Billing is itemised and the cancellation flow works in one click.

    How does the satisfaction guarantee differ from California Psychics?

    Psychic Source caps the refundable portion at 30 minutes (the rest falls under normal billing); California Psychics has no hard minute cap. In practice both handled our test complaints inside 24 hours.

    What is an Elite advisor?

    Elite is a long-tenure tier (5+ years on the platform, plus top-decile customer ratings). Rates reflect that: $9.99/min is typical. We tested two and both delivered high-specificity sessions.

    Can I use Psychic Source internationally?

    Yes — international phone readings connect via proxy. Some card issuers flag the first transaction as suspicious; contact your bank pre-emptively if you’re reading from outside the US.

    Does Psychic Source offer video?

    No, phone and chat only. For video, use Purple Garden or Oranum.
