Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best psychic reading app available right now?

Based on our extensive testing and user feedback, Keen stands out as the top psychic reading app of 2024. Their combination of strict advisor screening, high-quality mobile interface, and consistently accurate readings puts them ahead of competitors like Kasamba and Psychic Source.

Is the Sanctuary app worth using for readings?

While Sanctuary has beautiful design and good daily horoscopes, their live reading service is relatively limited compared to other apps. We recommend it for casual astrology enthusiasts who want daily insights, but serious seekers should look elsewhere for in-depth personal readings.

Are psychic app readings as accurate as desktop?

Yes, the accuracy of readings is the same whether you connect through a mobile app or desktop website. The only real difference is the interface – psychic advisors use the same methods and techniques regardless of how you connect to them.

Which app offers the best free trial for new users?

Kasamba currently offers the most generous trial with 3 free minutes plus 70% off your first reading, which is significantly better than most competitors. Their trial also lets you test multiple advisors rather than limiting you to just one, making it easier to find your ideal match.

Can I get a refund through an in-app purchase?

Most psychic apps process refunds through their own customer service teams rather than through Apple/Google Play stores. If you’re unsatisfied with a reading, you’ll need to contact the app’s support team directly – many offer satisfaction guarantees but usually require you to request the refund within 24-72 hours.

Subscriptions, Billing and Refunds

How do I cancel a subscription that began as a free trial?

Start in the store you used to sign up. On an iPhone, open your Apple ID settings and look for Subscriptions. On Android, open the Play Store, choose your profile, then Payments and subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Apple and Google both state that cancellations should generally be made before the renewal date, so check the end date on your trial.

Can Apple or Google refund an app purchase?

Both stores offer a request process for purchases made through them, and decisions are made case by case, so there is no guarantee. Refunds for purchases paid directly to the provider on its own checkout follow that provider’s terms instead. Keep your receipt, and if you cannot resolve a problem with the provider, your card issuer may offer a dispute route.

Why was I charged more than I expected?

Common causes include per-minute billing that continues until you disconnect, automatic top-ups, and introductory rates that revert to standard pricing. Check your account history and the offer wording first, then contact support in writing.

Privacy and Permissions

What permissions does a psychic app need?

Chat usually needs only notifications. Voice or video sessions need the microphone or camera. Contacts, photos and precise location are rarely necessary. Both iOS and Android let you review and revoke permissions in system settings at any time.

Where can I see how an app handles my data?

Store listings display summary privacy information, and the provider should publish a full privacy notice. Look for what is collected, how long it is kept and how to request deletion.

Reading Ratings and Reviews

Can I trust the star rating in an app store?

Treat it as a rough signal. Ratings reflect who chose to respond, and apps often ask for feedback after a pleasant moment. Read the recent, critical reviews as well, especially those mentioning billing or support. We cover this in our full app reviews.

Why do ranked lists disagree?

Each site uses its own criteria, and some earn commission from sign-ups. Check whether a site explains its method and discloses its income. Our shortlist method page describes ours.
